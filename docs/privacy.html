NearBaz — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NearBaz (“we”, “us”, “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information across our three mobile apps — NearBaz (customer), NearBaz Partner (shopkeeper), and NearBaz Rider (delivery rider) — and our website. This Privacy Policy is published by NearBaz (the operator of the apps and website).

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run a hyperlocal ordering & delivery service:

We do not collect card or bank credentials — UPI payments happen in your own UPI app; we only store the payment reference. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To show nearby shops, place and fulfil orders, and enable delivery & live tracking.
  • To send notifications about your orders / delivery jobs.
  • To verify delivery partners (KYC) and prevent fraud.
  • To provide customer support and keep legally required transaction records.

3. Location data

Location is used only while you are using the app (“when in use”). We do not track your location in the background. Customers use it to find shops and set a delivery point; riders share location while online/on an active delivery so customers and shops can track the order.

4. Sharing

We share the minimum necessary: a customer’s delivery location and name are shared with the assigned shop and rider for that order; a rider’s live location is shared with the customer and shop during an active delivery. We use the following service providers to operate the app: hosting/database and push-notification delivery (Expo / Google / Apple push services). We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within each app (Profile / Settings → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your personal profile data. We retain order, payment, and ledger records where required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes, in de-identified form where possible. See our account deletion page.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Login tokens are stored in the device’s secure keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Access to KYC documents is restricted to authorised admins.

7. Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Effective date” above reflects the latest version.
