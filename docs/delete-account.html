Delete your NearBaz account

You can delete your account and personal data directly inside any NearBaz app (Customer, Partner/Shopkeeper, or Rider).

In the app

  1. Open the NearBaz app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your personal profile data (name, phone, addresses, uploaded photos/KYC, device push tokens) is removed. Order, payment, and ledger records may be retained in de-identified form where required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes.

Can’t access the app? Email support@nearbaz.in from your registered address with the subject “Delete my account” and we will process the request.

See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and retain.
