# PassWaala — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 14 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how PassWaala ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses,
and protects your information across our three mobile apps — **PassWaala** (customer),
**PassWaala Partner** (shopkeeper), and **PassWaala Rider** (delivery rider) — and our website.

This Privacy Policy is published by **PassWaala** (the operator of the apps and website).

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run a hyperlocal ordering & delivery service:

| Data | Why we collect it | Apps |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone number** | Account identity and login | All |
| **Name** | To address you and label orders | All |
| **Precise location (GPS)** | Find nearby shops, set delivery address, match & track deliveries | Customer, Shopkeeper, Rider |
| **Delivery addresses** | Deliver your orders | Customer |
| **Photos you upload** | Product images & shop storefront (shopkeeper); delivery/UPI-payment proof | Shopkeeper, Rider |
| **KYC documents** (rider: ID, driving licence, etc.) | Legal verification of delivery partners | Rider |
| **UPI payment reference** (VPA / transaction ref) | Confirm UPI payments between customer and shop | All |
| **Order, delivery & ledger history** | Provide the service, support, and legal/accounting records | All |
| **Device push token** | Send order/job notifications | All |

We do **not** collect card or bank credentials — UPI payments happen in your own UPI app;
we only store the payment reference. We do **not** sell your data and we do **not** use it
for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 2. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- To show nearby shops, place and fulfil orders, and enable delivery & live tracking.
- To send notifications about your orders / delivery jobs.
- To verify delivery partners (KYC) and prevent fraud.
- To provide customer support and keep legally required transaction records.

## 3. Location data

Location is used **only while you are using the app** ("when in use"). We do not track your
location in the background. Customers use it to find shops and set a delivery point; riders
share location while online/on an active delivery so customers and shops can track the order.

## 4. Sharing

We share the minimum necessary: a customer's delivery location and name are shared with the
assigned shop and rider for that order; a rider's live location is shared with the customer
and shop during an active delivery. We use the following service providers to operate the app:
hosting/database and push-notification delivery (Expo / Google / Apple push services).
We may disclose information if required by law.

## 5. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within each app (Profile / Settings → **Delete
account**). Deleting your account removes your personal profile data. We retain order, payment,
and ledger records where required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes, in de-identified form
where possible.

> **Play Store account-deletion URL:** Google requires a public web URL that explains how to
> request deletion. Host `https://passwaala.in/delete-account` (a ready-to-host page is provided
> at `docs/delete-account.html`) stating: users delete in-app via Profile/Settings → Delete
> account, or email `support@passwaala.in`.

## 6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Login tokens are stored in the device's secure keystore
(iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Access to KYC documents is restricted to authorised admins.

## 7. Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
